# Editable reconstruction capability library

Use a single RGB as a set of constraints, not a ground-truth 3-D blueprint. Hidden geometry, absolute scale, light/material decomposition and camera/layout are underdetermined. Block the room and large objects first; use a consistent arbitrary scale and distinguish observation from hypotheses. Inspect major silhouettes, semantic facing, occlusion, vanishing directions, aspect ratios and object gaps before decorative details. Iterate camera and layout together: a plausible camera can make an incorrect layout look right, and vice versa. Preserve variants and compare fixed-camera renders before changing the view. No independent transfer improvement has been established.

The Python module is imported inside Blender (add this directory to `sys.path`). No function renders. `hinge_pose` changes the pivot transform; `directional_material` creates a new material. Other functions query the evaluated scene. Update the view layer after scene or camera changes before projection. `capabilities.json` is the index.

## Capability Name: Joint camera/layout landmark fitting
**Applicable Conditions:** Visible room corners and object landmarks, especially wide-angle interiors. **Problem and Technical Hypotheses:** Framing, lens, positions, sizes and rotations interact. Image coordinates constrain projection but not unique depth. Observe front/back ordering and semantic face directions, not just point coordinates. **Executable Method:** `landmarks(scene,camera,points)` projects world-space points into normalized image coordinates, bottom-up; `landmark_error(projected,targets)` scores manually annotated targets `(x,y,weight)`. Start with room corners and major object endpoints, retain candidate cameras and layouts, adjust a small group at a time. Use occlusion and silhouette separately; error is not a complete image metric. **Verification Method:** render matched resolution and compare visible corners, overlap, negative space, object orientation, and crop; inspect an overhead view and multi-landmark changes. **Failure Conditions and Side Effects:** points outside frame, behind camera, hidden or wrongly paired can mislead; this function does not test occlusion. Joint fitting is non-unique. **Evidence and Knowledge Origin:** general projection knowledge; development camera transforms queried directly; historical camera adjustments are not evidence of independent improvement.

## Capability Name: Hinged planar assemblies and occlusion
**Applicable Conditions:** Door, lid, or other hinged foreground surface. **Problem and Technical Hypotheses:** A missing face may be hidden by a solid wall, edge-on, outside view, or occluded by another object. A broad projected face alone does not establish a valid hinge. **Executable Method:** Build leaf, panels and handle as children of one pivot at a jamb. Leave pivot unparented; `hinge_pose(pivot, hinge_world, angle_radians)` swings its local geometry around that anchor; `hinge_error(pivot,jamb_point)` reports anchor displacement. Model a real opening in the wall, or assemble wall pieces around it. Hold camera fixed while testing swing, then test camera separately. **Verification Method:** compare camera outlines and overlap, diagnose with wall temporarily hidden, and inspect top and side views, hinge alignment, clearance, leaf-to-wall and furniture intersections. Restore visibility. **Failure Conditions and Side Effects:** anchor distance alone does not prove correct hinge orientation, wall opening or collision-free sweep; near-camera clipping and camera movement alter silhouette. Do not promote a visually attractive dislocated hinge to general repair. **Evidence and Knowledge Origin:** six-run exploration, especially door candidates and diagnostic views; observed limitations and rejected claims are detailed outside library.

## Capability Name: Directional material and light separation
**Applicable Conditions:** Surfaces with directional grain, veining or mottling. **Problem and Technical Hypotheses:** Surface color pattern and illumination/highlight can look similar. Coordinate frame and anisotropic scale determine apparent pattern direction and frequency. **Executable Method:** `directional_material(name,scale,colors,roughness,coordinate)` creates a new editable Texture Coordinate → Mapping → Noise → ramp → base color network. Assign to chosen mesh slot; tune scale, colors and mapping in local coordinates. `Generated` depends on object bounds; `Object` uses object coordinates; `UV` requires a suitable UV map. Compare with plain-color roughness-only and, when appropriate, a separately authored image texture. Do not assume this noise is marble or reproduces the reference. **Verification Method:** render equal camera/exposure/lighting variants, compare surface crop, direction, contrast, edge stretching, and nearby objects. Repeat after moving light only: highlights move, fixed albedo features remain attached to surface, though illumination affects their visibility. **Failure Conditions and Side Effects:** generated coordinates stretch with aspect ratio, UV seams and side faces may distort, changes of light also alter shadows. Pattern complexity does not imply accuracy. **Evidence and Knowledge Origin:** Blender procedural mapping knowledge and the historical paired-light comparisons; direct GT node queries demonstrate various material bindings, not a unique ideal technique.

## Capability Name: Support and connectivity screening
**Applicable Conditions:** Objects apparently resting on a floor or supported by another part. **Problem and Technical Hypotheses:** Floating, near contact, intersections, and disconnected assemblies are distinct. A global lowest point or AABB overlap does not verify all wheels or local supports. **Executable Method:** call `support_samples(object,support,tolerance,max_distance,sample_limit)` per explicitly selected support piece on evaluated mesh and surface. It samples lowest-band vertices and casts downward from slightly above; states are positive gap, near-zero, possible penetration or unknown support. Use `bounds_separation(a,b)` only as a broad-phase screen for potential connections, never as a contact test. Compare alternate whole-assembly translation versus localized geometry revisions, preserving pivots and seat clearance. **Verification Method:** inspect each intended contact in side views, cast or inspect additional relevant points, check for penetrations and separately inspect wheel–fork, fork–spoke, post–seat and other connections; rerun all tests after a repair. **Failure Conditions and Side Effects:** vertex sampling misses face interiors and non-lowest contacts, cannot reliably detect deep penetration, thin surfaces, overhangs, sparse meshes or unsupported regions. Ray hits are not force stability. Near-zero or AABB overlap is not proof of physical contact or assembly connectivity. Changing object heights can worsen silhouette and proportions. **Evidence and Knowledge Origin:** observed historical support tests and comparative chair edits; screening and uncertainty bounds are general technical knowledge.
