# Editable scene reconstruction library

Use these units together, not as a checklist of independent optimizations. A single RGB view does not identify absolute scale, hidden geometry, or a unique camera. Start from a coherent editable blockout; prioritize composition and semantic relations over tiny gaps. Reference landmarks, visible occlusions, floor/wall junctions, dominant directions and material regions are observations; geometry, light and lens are hypotheses. Keep the original blend and candidate renders separately. All functions are in `reconstruct.py`, callable from Blender after adding `/app/library` to `sys.path`. Function coordinates are Blender world units unless stated otherwise. No transfer improvement has been independently established. Synthetic execution checks do not validate image matching, rendered appearance, collision clearance, or support in arbitrary scenes; these methods remain unvalidated for those outcomes. The selection-inspired probe is not a reconstruction or independent validation.

## Capability Name: Joint camera, layout and articulation reasoning
**Applicable Conditions:** One RGB, a blockout and identifiable image features. **Problem and Technical Hypotheses:** Position, lens, object dimensions and rotation all change projected placement and occlusion. A larger silhouette can be due to depth, width, viewing angle or camera; a chair front/back or door hinge must remain meaningful. **Executable Method:** Record image landmarks as normalized (x,y), origin bottom left. `landmark_errors(scene,camera,[(name,world_xyz,target_xy,weight),...])` returns projected coordinates, weighted image distances and behind-camera flags. `point_camera(camera,location,target,lens)` sets a candidate. Rank visible large edges and silhouettes, but inspect occlusion and orientation separately. Change camera position/target/lens in small batches, then layout and object proportions; compare consistent full-frame renders and save each candidate. `hinge_pose(hinge,child,hinge_world,closed_vector,angle)` rotates an assembly around an authored local hinge; child must already have leaf geometry offset from the origin. Use `hinge_report` with leaf hinge and jamb points to audit the structure. Both mutate transforms; duplicate first. **Verification Method:** Compare corners, slopes, relative widths and occlusion ordering in aligned full-frame renders; view top/side for hinge, wall opening, leaf swing and intersections. Recheck full frame after each repair. **Failure Conditions and Side Effects:** Landmark matches are not visibility or silhouette tests; camera fitting is underconstrained and changing focal length changes perspective. Hinge distance does not prove collision-free motion, opening width or actual physical connection. Never enlarge or translate a leaf solely to fit a silhouette if its jamb/opening is incoherent. **Evidence and Knowledge Origin:** Camera revisions and repeated rendered articulation candidates appear in strategy trajectories; the simultaneous constraints and hinge audit are general technical hypotheses, not historically validated repairs. See external research evidence.

## Capability Name: Material coordinates, direction and lighting separation
**Applicable Conditions:** An editable planar or volumetric surface with visible structured appearance. **Problem and Technical Hypotheses:** Large directional streaks may be material, illumination, reflections, or geometry. Different coordinate frames make a texture move or change scale under transforms. **Executable Method:** `mapped_stone(name,scale,low,high,coord_object)` returns a new Blender material with object coordinates, anisotropically scaled noise and a color ramp. Assign it explicitly to the target slot. Smaller scale along an axis stretches pattern in that coordinate frame; use a shared coordinate reference object for continuity across pieces, or the default object coordinates for a single piece. This is a controllable starting hypothesis, not a marble guarantee. Lock exposure, camera, light, color management and object geometry; first match broad color, then direction, feature scale and contrast using corresponding reference and render crops. To test illumination, render a neutral diffuse material under the same lighting and compare; retain physically plausible local lamps rather than baking their glow into all surfaces. Tune only a few variables at a time. **Verification Method:** Inspect full view and matched crops, including top-to-edge continuity and visible texture direction; check again under final lighting and at final resolution. **Failure Conditions and Side Effects:** Procedural noise can look cloudy, and screen direction depends on camera and object orientation; insufficient sampling, saturation and reflections can conceal a pattern. Node complexity alone does not demonstrate improvement. Material creation changes bpy data but never assigns it. **Evidence and Knowledge Origin:** Two historical runs replaced separate vein geometry with mapped procedural material; only one records a crop comparison, which reported weaker features. This implementation is a new simplified hypothesis, not a copied or proven method.

## Capability Name: Support and assembly diagnostics
**Applicable Conditions:** Editable evaluated meshes and explicit plausible support objects. **Problem and Technical Hypotheses:** Floating, penetration and disconnected components can be hidden in the main view. Support and attachment are distinct relations. **Executable Method:** `support_probe(obj,support_objects,tolerance,samples,reach)` samples downward rays from above evaluated lowest vertices against only the supplied candidate meshes. It returns per-point signed vertical gaps: positive gap, near-zero, possible penetration, or unknown support; mixed samples remain mixed. Select tolerance and reach relative to scene scale. `aabb_relation(a,b)` returns evaluated world AABB separations and overlap as broad-phase triage for assembly pairs. **Verification Method:** Diagnose expected pairs (leg/floor, caster/fork, stem/seat, door/jamb), inspect side and top views and perform mesh-level proximity/intersection checks as appropriate; after changing geometry rerun probes and rerender the main camera. **Failure Conditions and Side Effects:** Near-zero or AABB overlap is not proof of contact, load bearing, connectivity or absence of intersection. Rays can miss narrow geometry, hit an overhanging or upper surface, or miss supports beyond reach; low vertices may not sample curved footprints. Negative gaps indicate only possible penetration. Unknown support must not be reported as zero. Read-only diagnostics do not repair geometry. **Evidence and Knowledge Origin:** Historical caster height measurements, floor ray checks and side views motivate the diagnostics; their measurements do not prove physical contact. The broader sampled probe and interpretation limits are new technical design.
