M1's teacher similarly says major objects and some relationships existed, including the wall band; it claims weak island-on-cabinet occlusion and incomplete background framing. These are recorded visual assessments, not independently verified here. The supplied prior code excerpts support existence of some named geometry, but are explicitly truncated and cannot establish full visual coverage. Sources: `P/tasks/E02_M0_R2/guidance.json`, `observations`; `P/tasks/E02_M1_R2/guidance.json`, `observations`; both corresponding `teacher_context.json`, `previous.bounded_trace`.

This supports object construction, not recognizability or correct occlusion.
- **Verified result:** No supplied post-R2 visual assessment establishes whether the target coverage or occlusion was achieved.

M1 makes a similar claim for its own preceding result, adding inadequate cabinet cropping and rightward extension. These are teacher image claims. M1 couples tabletop corners, visible faces, overhang, cabinet boundaries and occlusion. Sources: `P/tasks/E02_M0_R2/steps/001/build.py` lines 39–64 and 175–177; `P/tasks/E02_M0_R2/build.py` lines 21–39 and 105–107; `P/tasks/E02_M1_R2/steps/001/build.py` lines 36–39 and 144–146; `P/tasks/E02_M1_R2/build.py` lines 29–30 and 113–115; `P/tasks/E02_M1_R2/student_decision_2.json`, `reason`.

These are visual claims; M0's incomplete prior excerpt does support floorboard geometry of width `.566`, but does not establish the appearance or a unique material/exposure cause. Sources: `P/tasks/E02_M0_R2/build.py` lines 12–19, 51–59, 90–100; `P/tasks/E02_M1_R2/build.py` lines 5–21, 29–32, 70–76 and 99–103.

### Camera, lighting and rendering

Both hints preserve soft side lighting; M0 also says not to solve projection merely by increasing downward angle. M1's preceding diagnosis explicitly cautions against attributing appearance to a unique camera, material or light setting. In R2, both programs use a perspective camera and area lights; M1's second build reduces the second light's power from 420 to 250 and darkens assigned colors. Those are adopted program changes, not evidence that illumination or exposure matches the reference. Sources: both `guidance.json`, `hint` and `observations`; `P/tasks/E02_M1_R2/steps/001/build.py` lines 141–146; `P/tasks/E02_M1_R2/build.py` lines 110–115; both `camera.json`, `type` and `lens`.

## Technical outcome and limitations

Both R2 status files report two builds, selected step 2, no failure reason, `quality_verdict: null`, and `technical_success_only: true`; each says only step 1 was attached for student review. `state: not_started` coexists with `status: completed`, so these fields should not be silently harmonized. Stage records independently show selected-step build, save, reload and render completion, using Cycles CPU at 768×576 and 32 samples: `P/tasks/E02_M0_R2/steps/002/stage_events.jsonl` events 4–13; `P/tasks/E02_M1_R2/steps/002/stage_events.jsonl` events 4–13. The earlier teacher contexts also report successful previous steps, not an execution failure requiring repair. M1's teacher mentions a path-reading “-” notice while stating the result image was available; the underlying notice is not independently supplied. No broader technical-failure or experiment-wide conclusion is warranted.

**Bottom line:** the records demonstrate prompt-policy revision, two fresh guided builds, concrete partial adoption, and technical rendering success. They do not demonstrate visual correction, causal superiority of M1, generalization, or true learned reconstruction improvement.
