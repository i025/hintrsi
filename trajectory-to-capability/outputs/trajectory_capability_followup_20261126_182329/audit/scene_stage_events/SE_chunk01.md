**Evidence limit.** These selected records do not document teacher advice, student compliance with specific advice, pose, camera, lighting or material checks, physical contact, visual fidelity, or an acceptance decision. Nor do they show an actionable command transcript from which to distinguish a failed command from a merely proposed one. Render completion, object counts, zero modifiers, and any hypothetical `ok` flag or AABB overlap are not substitutes for those findings. The observed sequences are case-specific and should not be generalized to all scenes.
