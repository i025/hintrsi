## Cross-case interpretation

**Process completion is not acceptance.** Endpoint students visibly patched and passed their local suites; H0221 instead established a failing baseline, and H0227 stopped at diagnosis. H0252–H0253 show failing functional checks despite successful session termination. H0229 stopped before any visible action. These outcomes should not be pooled as either uniformly successful or uniformly algorithmically failed. No observed branch or CLI stop is established in these reports: H0230 and H0249 successfully created branches, while H0229’s exit -11 is an SDK command interruption, with cause unknown.

**Advice availability, receipt, diagnosis and execution are separate.** H0223, H0244, H0246 and H0250 establish teacher artifact creation, not delivery. H0222 and H0245 record a teacher check but expose no teacher message; H0256 explicitly reports guidance absent. Reviewer “USED” classifications in H0037, H0224 and H0247 establish perceived alignment, not receipt or causal influence. H0221’s diagnosis was informed by a reference diff, not independently discovered solely from testing. No causal superiority of teacher guidance is established.

**Transferable constraints and counterexamples.** Boundary-focused tests can expose a selection error affecting several aggregates, and validation must concern the executed change: the endpoint suite provides stronger case-specific evidence than H0232’s addition-only check for a new multiplication function. H0255 is a counterexample to treating all resume checks as similarly incomplete, because it directly exercised multiplication as well. A write acknowledgement is weaker than readback, and a session status is weaker than test output. The inclusive endpoint, arithmetic operators, shard totals, and parser rewrite are task-specific facts, not general prescriptions. Unverified hypotheses include whether unseen guidance influenced students, whether the endpoint fixes satisfy unstated requirements, and whether H0230’s rewritten headers affect diagnostic positions.

**Scene and missing data.** No Scene task, image, geometry, or render is evidenced in this group. Scene teacher detection, diagnosis, update, acceptance, and independently observed visual quality are therefore **not assessable**, rather than negative findings. Missing formal scores and hidden evaluations are unknown. Absent usage data cannot recover historical model cost. This synthesis is audit-only and does not enter capability generation.
