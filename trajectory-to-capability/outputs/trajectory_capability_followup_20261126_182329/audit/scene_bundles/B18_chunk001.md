- **Technical versus quality failure.** The C1 teacher reported one reconstruction with no final artifacts after an invalid fillet radius, a 600-second timeout, and a missing-world exception; its final correction was not executed. Primitive expansion was a plausible timeout hypothesis, not an established cause. The teacher also reported a completed reconstruction with 426 finite meshes, passing prescribed edits but scoring 0.3965542485 overall. - **Material and texture.** C2 attributed a missed material filter to semantic names on parent EMPTY markers rather than primitive mesh names, and hypothesized that per-mesh texture coordinates on scaled floor geometry explain an oversized pattern. The reported contribution to scores is unmeasured (`C2/proposal_result.json`, `diagnosis`; `C2/policy.py`, `PROCEDURE`, ranges above). These records do not independently establish the appearance of the rendered floor.
- **Pose, camera, light and render.** C2's proposed procedure separates camera pose/framing from local geometry and appearance, and compares brightness, texture and light direction with camera and geometry fixed. It treats camera hypotheses as estimates, not calibration, and forbids changing fixed renderer settings merely because of noise (`C2/policy.py`, `PROCEDURE`, [0,14972)).
