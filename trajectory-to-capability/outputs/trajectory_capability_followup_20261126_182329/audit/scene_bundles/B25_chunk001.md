## Acceptance and limitations

The policy’s usable-render rule is a technical validity gate, not a quality acceptance rule. Its `REVIEW` and `_selection_content` ask for comparison on framing, silhouettes, openings, occlusion, color regions and editable structure, without assuming the newest build is best (`policy.py` [0,13300)). The fixed evaluator’s actual thresholds and a candidate-acceptance decision are not supplied. The proposal reports passing structure/material/composite checks and non-target preservation, but these are proposal claims in this selection, not independently inspectable acceptance records. Neither those claims nor successful rendering would establish semantic completeness, physical contact, texture fidelity or metric 3D accuracy.
