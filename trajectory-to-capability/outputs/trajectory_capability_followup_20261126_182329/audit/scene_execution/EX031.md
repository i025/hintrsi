Each bunk comprises rod posts and rails, ball caps, box mattresses, covers and pillows, and a rod ladder. The three left bunks are rotated 90 degrees in the later build; a separate wooden panel accompanies the cropped foreground bunk. These are editable primitive assemblies, not evidence of realistic cloth or verified physical joints. [X0597, `build.py`, lines 32–91]
