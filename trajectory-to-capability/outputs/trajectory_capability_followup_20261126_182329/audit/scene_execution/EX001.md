## Generated scene and execution

The completed development scene’s selected build represents a French sitting room: boxed architecture and individual herringbone parquet boards; an antique chaise assembled from a filleted seat and underframe, swept apron, four swept legs and feet, head and arm frames; separate upholstered panels, pillow, card, partial cane chair and curtains. The pillow is rotated about two axes and the chair about Z. These are code-defined parts and placements, not proof of physical contact or faithful appearance. The build assigns procedural linen: high-frequency noise bump, with thresholded X/Y wave bands mixed into rose-colored dashes for patterned upholstery; the pillow gets plain linen. Parquet meshes receive randomly chosen walnut materials using scaled generated coordinates, noise and bump. These are shader operations in code, not a measured guarantee that grain or motifs are visible. [X0125, same file and archive, lines 228–284, 313–340]

The intended camera is at `(1.22, -4.90, 3.65)`, aimed at `(.12, .08, .96)`, 48 mm lens and −5° roll. The code sets a 0.20-strength world, warm 390-power and cool 65-power area emitters, 512×384 PNG, Cycles, 48 samples, denoising, AgX and −0.25 exposure. These are build settings, not independently measured final-render metadata. [X0125, same file and archive, lines 12–20, 342–366]
