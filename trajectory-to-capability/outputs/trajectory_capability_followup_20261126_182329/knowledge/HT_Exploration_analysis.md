## Applicability and evidence limits

These practices transfer to streamed workflows, recursive orchestration, and any system where a mock can satisfy an internal contract while an external adapter cannot produce it. Stronger verification would retain targeted regression tests and exercise negative paths, repeated or nested calls, fragmented streams, cancellation, and end-to-end continuation through each supported integration. The visible record does not demonstrate all of those checks.

The formal outcome reports 7/7 improvement checks and 31/31 preservation checks passed, with no evaluation timeout. That is an observed formal pass, **not proof of learning or exhaustive correctness**. The single available trajectory is consistent with the Hint’s requested investigation, but exposure and subsequent behavior do not establish that the Hint caused the change. The missing comparison evaluation after timeout is not a normal zero; no causal or state-alignment advantage can be inferred from it.
